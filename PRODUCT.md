## Platform

web

## Users

Kamil, a concert and documentary photographer working under the name **buczakaf** from Jarosław, Poland, is the subject and sole author of the site. The primary audience is people who encounter his photography — event organizers, bands and artists, and the local live-music and scouting community — and come to see the body of work as a whole. The site's job for them is to form a clear impression of who Kamil is as a photographer and how his images feel.

Confirmed as a **personal career showcase**: presentation first, conversion second. A visitor should leave having seen the work, not having been sold to. A contact path still exists for anyone who wants to reach out (the Kontakt page, Instagram, and email), but booking is not the primary framing of the site.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Kamil's concert and documentary photography, documents the events he has covered, and communicates his identity as a photographer. Success means a visitor understands his craft and his presence in the local live-music scene, and can reach him if they want to.

## Positioning

**Under-the-stage intensity.** Kamil's differentiator is the ability to capture live energy and real emotion from the pit and the front of the stage — the intensity of being close to the performance rather than photographing it from a distance. This is the truthful claim the work is built to demonstrate and the impression the site must earn.

## Operating Context

- Kamil has been shooting since April 2024, with concerts and live events as the central subject (**confirmed** in the About copy).
- Coverage so far spans concerts, festivals, band sessions, and community/ceremonial media service (Dni Jarosławia 2025 and 2026, III Jarosławskie Juwenalia PANS, Śpiewogranie 2026, HSL Lednica, 1. Jarosławska Drużyna Harcerzy „Pościg”, and ongoing work with the band Medeis).
- The information the audience looks for is the images themselves, organized by event and artist, plus a short biography and a way to make contact.
- Bilingual by default: Polish is the default language, with an English translation available.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static multi-page site in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no build step or framework.
- Three pages: Portfolio (`index.html`), O mnie (`o-mnie.html`), Kontakt (`kontakt.html`).
- Portfolio is filtered by event with a per-event artist navigation; images open in a lightbox with keyboard and swipe navigation.
- Language switcher toggles PL/EN and persists the choice in `localStorage`.
- Image-forward by necessity: the site's substance is the photograph library.

## Brand Commitments

- The lowercase wordmark **buczakaf** is the identity and must be preserved.
- Bilingual **PL + EN** with Polish as the default is a binding commitment.
- **Instagram (@buczakaf)** is the primary public channel, alongside email **buczakaf@gmail.com**.
- Voice: first-person, direct, and unpretentious.

## Product Principles

1. **Let the photographs lead.** The work is the argument; interface and copy exist to present it without competing for attention.
2. **Proximity over polish.** The "under-the-stage" perspective is the point — keep the energy of live photography legible rather than sanitizing it.
3. **Show the real record.** Only actual events, artists, and images may be presented; nothing invented to fill a gap.
4. **Personal, not corporate.** A first-person showcase of one photographer, not an agency storefront.
5. **Bilingual by default.** Polish-first with a genuine English equivalent, never a translation afterthought.
